    Quote

    Quotative

    The reporting cue outside the quote (said, told, according to). Attribution can also be implicit — no cue written. That is a valid label, not a skip.

    Speaker

    Select the minimal person span (Obama, she). Mark the person’s first mention in the article (required) and optionally an introductory phrase. If a mention is selected, Intro phrase / First span apply to that mention instead. If the speaker span is a pronoun, keep it and link Wikidata by typing the name or clicking a candidate below.

    Mentions in the quote

    Entities referred to inside this quote (people, organisations, places, and so on). Select a span in the highlighted quote, then Add mention. Empty is fine when nothing is mentioned. Each mention also needs a first span in the article; select the mention, then First span (F). Intro phrase is optional. Reuse chips copy a span already marked for the same entity.

      Notes

      What to annotate

      • Direct quotes only. Every span between quotation marks is a candidate; keep real speech and reject titles, scare quotes, and non-speech.
      • Interrupted quotations are one unit. If the journalist splits speech around a reporting clause — “QUOTE_1,” remarked SPEAKER, “QUOTE_2.” — use Merge next (G) so both spans share one speaker, quotative, and mention list. Unmerge (U) splits them again. Do not reject the second span as a duplicate.
      • Do not annotate the whole article for entities. Only the speaker and mentions inside kept quotes. Selecting a span softly highlights the other occurrences of that same string in the article.
      • Quotative: the journalist’s reporting cue (said, told, according to), or Implicit when attribution is present with no written cue. Implicit is a label, not a missing value. Not the sentiment of the speaker.
      • Speaker: the source of the quote, as a minimal person span. If that person cannot be identified, mark Cannot identify speaker — still keep or reject the quote; do not skip it.
      • Intro phrase (optional): a role or description that introduces the entity, such as the president of the US. Select it in the article, then Intro phrase (P). Applies to the speaker, or to the selected mention. Does not replace the speaker/mention span.
      • First span (required for identified speakers and for every mention): the first mention of that entity in the article. Select it, then First span (F). Often a full name; may be the same as the speaker span. Click a reuse chip to copy a span already marked on another speaker or mention.
      • Pronouns: keep the pronoun as the speaker/mention span and link it to Wikidata (type the name, or click a nearby name / already-linked entity). Do not mark a separate referent span.
      • Mentions: entities inside the quote (people, organisations, places; names, pronouns, nominals). Link each to Wikidata or mark not in Wikidata. Click a QID to open the Wikidata page. Use the Google and Wikidata icons next to Not in Wikidata to look a name up. Any Wikidata item is valid; do not skip non-person entities. Linking a QID already used in this article also copies its intro phrase and first span when those fields are still empty.
      • Sentiment: the speaker’s attitude toward that mention as expressed in the quote (positive, negative, mixed, neutral, or not about them).
