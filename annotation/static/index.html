      Notes

      What to annotate

      • Direct quotes only. Every span between quotation marks is a candidate; keep real speech and reject titles, scare quotes, and non-speech.
      • Do not annotate the whole article for entities. Only the speaker and mentions inside kept quotes.
      • Quotative: the journalist’s reporting cue (said, told, according to), or Implicit when attribution is present with no written cue. Implicit is a label, not a missing value. Not the sentiment of the speaker.
      • Speaker: the source of the quote, as a minimal person span. If that person cannot be identified, mark Cannot identify speaker — still keep or reject the quote; do not skip it.
      • Pronouns: keep the pronoun as the speaker/mention span and link it to Wikidata (type the name, or click a nearby name / already-linked entity). Do not mark a separate referent span.
      • Mentions: entities inside the quote (people, organisations, places; names, pronouns, nominals). Link each to Wikidata or mark not in Wikidata. Any Wikidata item is valid; do not skip non-person entities.
      • Sentiment: the speaker’s attitude toward that mention as expressed in the quote (positive, negative, mixed, neutral, or not about them).
